Lasagna

Description

This is a juicy recipe for Lasagna. Be careful bringing this to Thanksgiving because your whole family may fight for the last piece.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 1 can tomato sauce
  • 1 can tomato paste
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 12 ounces cottage cheese
  • 1/2 cup grate Parmesan cheese
  • 1 package lasagna noodles

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Cook and stir ground beef, onion, and garlic in a large skillet over medium heat until meat is crumbly and browned.
  3. Add tomato sauce, tomato paste, salt, and oregano; stir until well combined and warmed through. Remove from heat.
  4. Stir mozzarella, cottage cheese, and Parmesan together in a large bowl until well combined. Set aside.
  5. Spread a layer of meat sauce onto the bottom of a slow cooker.
  6. Add a double layer of uncooked lasagna noodles, breaking noodles to fit into the cooker as needed.
  7. Top noodles with a portion of cheese mixture
  8. Repeat the layering of sauce, noodles, and cheese until all of the ingredients are used.
  9. Cover and cook on Low until noodles are tender, 4 to 6 hours.
